THREE TREATMENTS, TEN MOTIONS

click any specimen to replay
A · INKrestrained

Pure lettering. No shapes, no framing. A bone-white face, heavy black outline, one flat red drop shadow offset down-right, sheared eight degrees. The oldest trick in the printed comic and the only one that survives at small sizes.

Face — Passion One 900
Stroke — 5.5% of cap height, #0A0A09
Shadow — flat, 0.09em ×2, red then black
Motion — omPop, 240ms, overshoot 1.15

B · BURSTloudest

The word sits inside a jagged clip-path burst, with a teal plate misregistered behind it the way a cheap four-colour press would drop it. Slams in from oversize and shakes off the impact.

Face — Rubik Mono One
Shape — 22-point clip-path, 2 plates
Offset — 7px teal, off-register
Motion — omSlam 320ms + omShake 200ms

C · SHREDfastest

Manga speed lines. The word is stretched vertically, sheared hard, and thrown in from off-screen left in 200ms with a red ghost trailing a frame behind. Built for motion words, not impacts.

Face — Anton, scaleY 1.35
Lines — repeating-linear-gradient, masked
Ghost — red copy, 10px lag
Motion — omSlide, 200ms, ease-out

LIGHT
local punctuation

Sits against the line that caused it, roughly cap-height ×2.5. Never overlaps text the player is still reading. The composition does not move. A door, a safety catch, a phone buzzing in a coat.

Size — 38px
Enter — 220ms
Hold — 500ms
Disruption — none
MEDIUM
breaks the composition

Lands over the reading area at an angle, dims the prose behind it to 45%, and clears. Reading is interrupted for about a second. A punch that connects, a bottle going through a window.

Size — 104px
Enter — 300ms
Hold — 820ms
Disruption — dim + tilt
HEAVY
owns the viewport

Full bleed. The screen shakes, a halftone wash blows out the panel, and nothing else is legible until it goes. Reserve it — two or three times in a chapter, or it stops meaning anything.

Size — fills width
Enter — 440ms
Hold — 1100ms
Disruption — shake + wash

Ten keyframes, three type recipes, three intensity tiers. Every effect is real text in the DOM, so it is selectable, translatable, and readable by a screen reader. Nothing here uses canvas, SVG animation or particles. Under prefers-reduced-motion the entrances collapse to a 90ms fade and the screen shake is dropped.
