# Brief for Claude Design — comic sound-effect sticker pack

Design a set of eight comic-book sound-effect stickers for a LINE messaging
sticker pack. One artboard per sticker, each exactly **320 × 270 px**, on a
**transparent background**.

These are static designs — the animation is added later. Draw each sticker as
it looks at rest, at the moment it has fully landed.

## The eight stickers

Each one is a different conversational reaction, not a variation on the same
impact. The word is the whole design; there are no characters or illustrations.

| Word | What a person is saying by sending it |
|---|---|
| BOOM! | nailed it, mic drop |
| POW! | burn, take that |
| OOF! | that hurt, cringe |
| YIKES! | awkward |
| ZAP! | done, and fast |
| ARGH! | frustration |
| ZZZ… | boring, asleep |
| HUH? | confused |

## Visual direction

Classic 1960s comic-book sound effects — the BIFF / POW / ZAP lettering people
picture from Batman, pop art and vintage comic covers. Loud, primary, fun.
Not sleek, not minimal, not modern-flat.

**Palette**
- Yellow `#FFD629` — the burst ground and most lettering
- Red `#E52521` — the ring inside the outline, and lettering on bursts
- Black `#0A0A09` — every outline, heavy
- White `#FFFFFF` — the inner ring around letters
- Electric cyan `#3ACBF0` — ZAP! only, so the set is not eight yellows

**Lettering**
Heavy condensed display type, all caps, slightly italic or skewed so it leans
into the motion. Every word gets a thick black outline with a thinner white
ring inside it, so it reads on both light and dark chat backgrounds.

**Two treatments — mix them across the set**
1. **Burst** — the word sits inside a jagged starburst: yellow centre, thin red
   ring, heavy black outline, halftone dot texture over the yellow. Use for the
   loudest words (BOOM!, POW!).
2. **Plain lettering** — no shape behind it, just the word with its outlines and
   a hard offset drop shadow in red. Use for the rest.

Punctuation is part of the design — the exclamation marks and the question mark
carry as much weight as the letters. ZZZ… should read as three Z's rising
diagonally, each larger than the last.

## Rules

- Fill the frame. A sticker is viewed at roughly 180px wide on a phone, so thin
  or small lettering disappears. The word should nearly touch the edges.
- Nothing may be clipped by the 320 × 270 boundary.
- Transparent background — no panel, no card, no border.
- No gradients on the letters. Flat colour, hard edges, like printed ink.
- Test each design mentally against a **white chat** and a **dark chat**. The
  black outline is what makes that work; do not drop it.

## What good looks like

Someone glances at the sticker for half a second in a group chat and instantly
knows both the word and the feeling. Legibility beats detail every time.
